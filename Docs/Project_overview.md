# ZEFA_ANALYTICA 254-PROJECT OVERVIEW

INTRODUCTION

ZEFA Analytica is a geospatial risk intelligence and predictive spatial analytics initiative aimed at supporting environmental monitoring,spatial pattern recognition,risk assessment and evidence based decision making.
The project seeks to combine geospatial technologies,data analytics,statistical methods and artificial intelligence to better understand spatial patterns and emerging risks affecting communities,infrastructure and environmental systems.

VISION
To develop an accessible geospatial intelligence platform capable of identifying,visualizing and predicting spatial risks for planners,researchers,organizations and decision makers.

PROBLEM STATEMENT
Many environmental and infrastructure related risks are difficult to identify early due to fragmented data,limited analytical tools and insufficient integration of spatial intelligence into decision making processes.
ZEFA Analytica seeks to address this challenge by transforming spatial data into actionable insights.

OBJECTIVES
Explore geospatial risk intelligence methodologies.
Develop practical GIS and spatial analytics workflows.
Build predictive analytical capabilities using statistical and machine learning techniques.
Support evidence-based planning and risk management.
Create a learning and experimentation platform for geospatial analytics.

CURRENT STATUS
This project is currently in the foundation and research phase.
Current areas of focus include:
GIS and QGIS fundamentals.
Spatial data management.
Geodatabases.
Statistics and risk analysis.
Python for geospatial workflows.
Project documentation and knowledge management.
